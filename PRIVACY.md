# Privacy Policy — Squeeze Compressor

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Squeeze Compressor is built to work without an account, without ads and without tracking. This page explains,
in plain language, what the app does with your data.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party
tracking library in this app. We do not have a server that stores your content, and we do not
build a profile of you.

## Network access

This app does not request the INTERNET permission. Everything it does happens on your device, and nothing you do in the app leaves it.

## What is stored, and where

Anything the app remembers — settings, history, files you create or import — is stored in the
app's own storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing
its data from Android settings, removes it.

## Permissions

The app asks only for the permissions its features need, and only when you use those features.
Whatever sits behind a permission (camera, microphone, storage, contacts, phone state) is read on
the device for that feature and is never uploaded.

## Third-party services

Squeeze Compressor is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any of the services it can display or
download content from. If you use a feature that fetches content from another service, that
service's own privacy policy applies to that request.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be committed to this repository, and the date at
the top of this page will change with it.
